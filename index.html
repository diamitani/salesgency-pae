(FAQs)

Your Questions, Answered

Everything you need to know about our approach
Why SalesGency instead of hiring a full-time SDR? +
A full-time SDR costs $85k–$110k annually (salary, benefits, tool stack) and spends up to 70% of their time on manual copy-pasting, prospecting, and CRM data entry. SalesGency automates the entire bottom-of-the-funnel grunt work deterministically with AI agents and workflows — allowing your closers to focus 100% on high-value conversations and closed deals.
How fast does the engine launch? +
The Custom Build Package is available immediately upon checkout. The Custom Agent launches in 7 days after onboarding. A full Custom Engagement takes 3–4 weeks for complete discovery, bespoke webhook builds, CRM integration, and inbox warmup.
How do you protect domain reputation and prevent spam flags? +
We never send outbound from your primary corporate email domain. We configure dedicated secondary domains with SPF, DKIM, DMARC, and Custom Tracking Domains (CTD), paired with a 21-day algorithmic warmup schedule and strict volume caps per inbox (max 30 cold emails/day per inbox).
What tech stack do I need to provide? +
Our systems work with whatever you currently have. We natively support HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, Smartlead, Instantly, Clay, Apollo, and n8n. If you don't have these, we advise on the leanest and most cost-effective setup during onboarding.
Who owns the workflows and data? +
You own 100% of everything we build. All n8n workflows, prompt templates, sequence copies, and enriched contact lists belong exclusively to your company. No vendor lock-in ever.
